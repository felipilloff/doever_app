# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome different experience levels,
explain disagreements with evidence, and critique work rather than people.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, and unwanted sexual
attention are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation to protect the
community. Reports should be sent privately to repository maintainers through
GitHub. Share only the information needed to investigate; do not post private
personal information in public issues. Maintainers should handle reports fairly,
confidentially where practical, and without retaliation.
